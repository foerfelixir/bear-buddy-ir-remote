Firmware lineage

The NEC remote firmware derives from wagiminator's TinyRemote for CH32V003. Its CC BY-SA 3.0 notice and attribution are retained in the repository's LICENSE file.

The configurable and Samsung adaptations remain derivative firmware under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 terms. The bundled browser config codec in variants/configurable-irc2/web/ir.js is covered by the separate MIT notice in that directory.

License boundary

The CH32V003 firmware and firmware-derived documentation are CC BY-SA 3.0 unless explicitly stated otherwise. The browser-side IRC2 codec is MIT. The traced schematic and hardware notes are documented separately in the hardware repository.

Hardware evidence

The schematic in this archive was reverse-engineered by physically tracing PCB lines. It is not an official TomatoCube drawing. The hardware repository retains the original PDF and SHA-256 checksum.
